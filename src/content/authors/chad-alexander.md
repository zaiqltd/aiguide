Electrical and Information Engineering. Not a fallback, a bet: a frontier model ships every few
weeks, and a course outline is locked years before you write the exam.

business actually cares about. You bring a problem, he and co-founder Adam Sacharowitz aim the

On this guide he writes the reality-checks, the South African business angle, and how to get your
business found when a customer asks ChatGPT for the best in your category. Different problems, same
move.
