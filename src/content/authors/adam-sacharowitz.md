Adam Sacharowitz is co-founder of [Zaiq](https://zaiq.co.za), an AI engineering studio in
Johannesburg. He went all-in on AI in his third year of Electrical and Information Engineering at
Wits, because a new model lands every few weeks and resets what is possible, and you cannot learn
that on a timetable.

He is across the entire AI toolkit, not just the chatbots: the models, plus the voice, image, video
and automation tools, and he is on the new ones the day they ship. Idea to a working fix in a day is
the normal speed, not the exception.

On this guide he writes the build-it and how-it-ships side: how to automate the admin that eats your
week, what custom software should and should not cost in rand, and how to choose between WordPress,
custom, and no-code. Talk is cheap. The working thing is the proof.
