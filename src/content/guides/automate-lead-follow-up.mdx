A lead is hottest the second it arrives and cools fast. Most small businesses lose deals not on price
or pitch, but in the silent gap between someone enquiring and anyone replying. Close that gap and you
win business you are currently leaking. The good news: closing it is an automation problem, and a
solvable one.

<Callout variant="fact">
Responding to a lead in five minutes instead of thirty makes you about 100x more likely to reach them
(MIT and InsideSales research), and the first business to respond usually gets the deal. No human
replies reliably in seconds at every hour; a workflow does.
</Callout>

## The leak, in plain terms

It looks like this. An enquiry comes in at 8pm through your website form. You see it at 9am. By then
the customer has messaged three competitors and one of them replied at 8:04pm. You never had a
chance, and you never knew why you lost. Lead follow-up automation removes that failure mode entirely
by making the first reply instant and the follow-up relentless but polite.

## Build it, or use what is built

Off-the-shelf gets you a long way. A CRM's built-in sequences plus the WhatsApp Business app cover
many small businesses. You move to custom work when:

- Leads arrive from several places that do not connect cleanly, and you need them reliably merged.
- You want genuinely personalised, instant replies at volume, which usually means the WhatsApp
  Business API wired into your systems.
- The judgement matters: an assistant that reads the enquiry and drafts a relevant first reply, rather
  than a fixed template, is where a small amount of AI earns its place.

<Callout variant="take">
The first reply is 80% of the win. If you only automate one thing, automate an instant
acknowledgement on WhatsApp the moment a lead arrives. Everything else, qualification, sequences,
routing, is a bonus on top of closing that first gap.
</Callout>

## Where it goes wrong

- **Automating the follow-up but not the first reply.** The first reply is the expensive gap. Start
  there.
- **No single home for leads.** Enquiries scattered across inboxes and apps get dropped. One CRM or
  inbox first.
- **Letting the bot overstay.** The instant the lead engages, a human takes over. Automation that
  keeps talking over a live conversation costs you the goodwill you just bought.
