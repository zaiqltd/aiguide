One rule explains the whole table: you are paying for **scope and skill**, not for a category. A
"website" is R5,000 or R250,000 depending on what it has to do. Pin down the job first, then the
price makes sense.

## How to read any digital quote

1. **What is the job?** Get found, take bookings, sell, automate, or look credible. The job sets the
   scope and the scope sets the price.
2. **Build or run?** Build work is paid once and you should own it. Run work (SEO, ads, hosting) is
   monthly. Do not pay monthly for something that does not change.
3. **Who is doing it?** A DIY tool, a freelancer, an agency, and an engineering studio price the same
   outcome very differently, mostly because of overhead and markup.
4. **What is the two-year total?** A cheap build with heavy monthly lock-in often beats a fair build
   on light running costs. Add it up over two years before you sign.
