What the answer means

  • Named The engine returns your business. You are in the conversation. Now defend it.
  • Outranked A rival is named and you are not. The answer exists, the engine just does not trust yours yet.
  • Invisible You get a vague, generic answer and nobody like you is named. Nobody wrote the answer the engine wants.

The 4 levers that decide it

  • 01 Entity clarity The engine can tell who you are, where you are, and what you do, without guessing.
  • 02 Answer content Somewhere on the web, the exact answer to that question is written down, plainly.
  • 03 Trusted citations Sources the engine already trusts mention you: directories, reviews, the press.
  • 04 Freshness That information is current, so the engine is willing to repeat it today.

What decides whether AI names you

Four levers move your business into the answer. Most local businesses do none of them, which is why the engines name the same few competitors every time. It is not a popularity contest. It is an engineering problem, and it is winnable.

  • Entity clarity. The engine can confidently identify your business as one thing: one consistent name, description and details everywhere, with structured data on your site.
  • Answer content. You have published the answer to the question being asked, leading with a clear, quotable response.
  • Trusted citations. Sources the engine already trusts mention you: press, directories, credible third-party content.
  • Freshness. The information is current. Engines down-rank stale answers.

Questions people ask

How do I check if ChatGPT recommends my business?

Ask ChatGPT the question a buyer would ask, like "who is the best [your service] in [your city]?", then see whether your business is named. Repeat it on Google AI and Perplexity. If a competitor appears and you do not, you are outranked. If no specific business appears, the category is still up for grabs. This tool builds the exact prompts for you.

Why is my business invisible to AI search?

Usually not because you are small, but because nobody has written the clear, structured answer the engine wants to quote, and your business identity is inconsistent across the web. AI engines name businesses they can clearly identify and that are described in content they trust. Fix the identity and the answer content and you become quotable.

Does this tool send my business details anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser. Nothing you type is sent to us or anyone else. The tool simply builds the prompts you can paste into the AI engines yourself.
